# VCvJNVQ2II4 — Pre-session self-assessment

- Length: 00:42 | Views: 177 | Likes: 3 | Published: 2026-04-30

> Everyday before trading, you need to assess how you feel before the session.
> If you really feel the urge to take a trade that day, you can assess the situation actually stop and analyze if the market is moving the way it should move in order for you to open a trade or if it's better to wait and not force any setups. Or if you feel really anxious because you're about to get your first payout, you can make it your goal for that day to only take a trade if it's really obvious. So, by doing this exercise and becoming aware of this information, you'll be able to adjust yourself for that session accordingly so you can take better trades. And this is what's going to give you that mental edge that you need in order to be in control of your decisions while you're trading.

# SO1G1IGiIRo — Pattern recognition working against you

- Length: 00:22 | Views: 352 | Likes: 1 | Published: 2026-04-29

> The problem with taking a bad trade or a meet [meh] setup that ended up making you money is that your brain will start looking for something similar in the future. Through all time, you will end up losing because you're taking lower probability setups, but your brain has it registered as if they work.
> Your worst enemy in trading is your own pattern recognition working against you because of your own bad habits.

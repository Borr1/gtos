# 4-3Bf2s97F8 — Lucky-streak danger

- Length: 00:34 | Views: 83 | Published: ~2026-04

> If you took a very neat trade and it worked, that's probably one of the worst things that could happen to you as a trader because now your brain registers that trade as something that worked, right? So, now once you see a similar trade, your brain will tell you to take that trade and as you may know, in the long term, those trades will bring you more losses than wins. So, you may have a lucky streak with these trades, but in reality, you will realize with time in the charts that the only trades you should be taking are the ones that look pretty obvious.

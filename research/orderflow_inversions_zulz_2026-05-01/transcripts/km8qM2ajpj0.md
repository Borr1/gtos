- Length: 00:39 | Views: 57 | Published: ~2026-04

> If you're able to understand these one aspect of how the market moves, you'll be able to become profitable faster and avoid many, many losses as a day trader. And that aspect is the fact that there are some weeks in the market where your priority needs to be to not lose money and preserve your capital.
> And there are other weeks in the market where your priority should be to make money and be more aggressive. And this way harder than it sounds because as a human being, you want to make money. But if your priority is make money in one of those weeks where you should be trying to preserve your capital and be less aggressive, you'll end up losing more money than you have to.

> All right, guys. A lot of you have been asking me, "Why do I use Sierra Chart instead of TradingView in order to do my volume analysis and my volume profile analysis and to draw my low volume nodes?" And the reason behind this is because Sierra Chart uses a tick-by-tick analysis of volume and of orders that gives me the most accurate information possible. On the other hand, what TradingView does is an aggregation of a certain amount of ticks in order to do that volume analysis. So, this is why when I draw my low volume nodes using a Sierra Chart volume profile, they are a little bit different than the low volume nodes I get from a TradingView volume profile. And obviously, the ones I get from Sierra Chart are way more accurate, and those are the ones I use for my trading. This is the same exact reason why you guys shouldn't be using footprint charts from TradingView.

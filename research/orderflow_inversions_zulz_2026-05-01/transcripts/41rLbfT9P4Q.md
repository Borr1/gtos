# 41rLbfT9P4Q — Mentor doesn't determine your profitability

- Length: 00:32 | Views: 8 | Published: 2026-04-27

> There are unprofitable traders with 200,000 followers teaching you their strategy and the crazy thing is that they actually have a lot of profitable students. The mentor has nothing to do with your profitability. You are responsible to make yourself profitable and your mentor has nothing to do with that. Yes, you can have a mentor, a good mentor that makes you profitable faster, but if you become profitable or not depends entirely on you and nobody else.

# 2aM0djK6w5o — Try multiple strategies first 3 months

- Length: 00:34 | Views: 203 | Likes: 3 | Published: 2026-04-21

> I see a lot of traders saying that you need to find a single strategy since the beginning and just focus on that one and just stick to that one. In my opinion, if you're just starting out, you should dedicate like probably 3 months to learning a couple of different strategies so you can start getting information on what's the style of trading that you like the most. And once you gather that data and you know exactly how many hours a day you want to be trading, what kind of trades you want to be taking, how long you want to be holding your trades, once you have that data, you'll have the information that you need in order to choose the strategy that best fits you.

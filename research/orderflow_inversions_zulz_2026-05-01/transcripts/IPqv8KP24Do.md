# IPqv8KP24Do — Trading as a sport

- Length: 00:31 | Views: 29 | Published: 2026-04-28

> You guys need to begin approaching trading as if it was a sport. So if you play a sport, you know exactly what your role is. You know exactly what success looks like. You know exactly what the rules of the game are. And after every game, you actually have to stop and analyze what went right and what went wrong so that you can make adjustments that will make you better. Winning one game is not your goal, but your goal is to win consistently over the next 12 months. So that in the long term you are a winner and not a loser.

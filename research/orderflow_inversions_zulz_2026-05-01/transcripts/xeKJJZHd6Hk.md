- Length: 00:46 | Views: 157 | Likes: 1 | Published: 2026-04-30

> If you guys can sit in a room for 2 hours and do nothing, no phone, no music, no TV, no nothing, you guys cannot trade. Like actually do the exercise. Sit still in your room for 15 minutes, close your eyes and do not fall asleep. And you'll see that it will be uncomfortable at first, but it will be probably one of the best exercises you guys can do for your trading psychology because it will teach your brain to relax. Stop seeking that instant dopamine hit. That also applies to trading because when you open on trade, you get that same instant dopamine hit that you get by watching your phone, by scrolling on Instagram, by watching TV. And by teaching your brain to not seek that, you are building the mind of a profitable trader because you need to have a relaxed mind when you're trading.

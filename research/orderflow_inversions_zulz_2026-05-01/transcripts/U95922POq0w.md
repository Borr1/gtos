# U95922POq0w — Profitable trading is boring

- Length: 00:28 | Views: 1.0K | Likes: 7 | Published: 2026-04-28

> The more I trade, the more I realized that being a profitable day trader is actually kind of boring. If your sessions feel exciting to you, that's probably because you're doing something wrong. If you think about it, doing the things that actually make you profitable is boring.
> Respecting your rules is boring, being patient is boring, not over trading is definitely not exciting, but these are the things that will make you profitable and these are the things that you need to implement if you want to become profitable as fast as possible.

> I see a lot of you having trouble understanding what absorption actually is, so let me give you guys an example.
> When you see a lot of buyers buying at a certain level and they're not able to keep bringing price up, those buyers are getting absorbed. And that is a very important signal in trading because when sellers enter the market, they will be able to bring price down very easily. Once you're using real order flow tools like the footprint charts, it's very easy to see these things happening in real time and it will make your trades way high probability.

# oRuRCx7xjC4 — Lack of patience is your biggest problem

- Length: 00:37 | Views: 892 | Published: 2026-04-29

> Some people in trading are way too smart for their own good. They overcomplicate everything. They're using a bunch of different confluences and reasons to take or not take a trade, and they're putting themselves in this place of information overload, and this kills their accounts faster than anything. If you actually get to know profitable traders, you will realize that what they have in common is not intelligence, but patience. They're able to sit still and not take any trades when the market is not giving them any trades and be patient in market conditions that are not high probability for their strategy. That's the trait that really defines profitable traders.

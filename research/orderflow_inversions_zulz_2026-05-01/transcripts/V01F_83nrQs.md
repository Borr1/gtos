# V01F-83nrQs — Limbic system / nervous system regulation

- Length: 00:49 | Views: 191 | Likes: 2 | Published: 2026-04-22

> The reason why trading so hard is because your mind associates money to survival. When you're trading, you're trying to make money, right? But what ends up happening is that your limbic system predominates in your decision-making while you're trading because of your survival instincts. What you have to do is learn how to detach yourself from that outcome, which is money, and you need to learn how to regulate your nervous system so that when you're trading, you can take more rational decisions. Doing things every day that help you regulate your nervous system so that when you're trading, your prefrontal cortex predominates in your decision-making. This is the part of your brain that's responsible for reasoning, that's responsible for logical thinking, and it's the part of your brain that you need in order to become profitable.

> Your life also has to be an A+ setup.
> Organize your life in a way in which it will be easy for you to be calm while trading, in a way in which it will be easy for you to be patient and respect your rules. If you're desperate about money, if you're behind on bills, if you have problems on your life that take your mental headspace and do not allow you to trade calmly, it will be very hard for you to develop the calm headspace that you need in order to be profitable.

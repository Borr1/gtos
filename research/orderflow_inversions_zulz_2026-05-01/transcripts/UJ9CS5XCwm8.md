> ICT traders mark a fair value gap, but order flow traders mark a low volume node. If you mark out the fair value gap, it'll most of the times give you a huge zone, while if you mark out the low volume node inside that fair value gap, it'll give you a small zone within that fair value gap, and that's the exact zone where you will see price reacting from. You're using low volume nodes, you're improving your probability because these are backed by real volume and real orders.

# QGx3xlJsRVk — Discipline both ways

- Length: 00:19 | Views: 419 | Published: ~2026-04

> Your brain on a losing streak and your brain on a winning streak are both equally as dangerous. One of them will make you revenge trade and the other one will make you size up recklessly and they are both equally as dangerous for your trading accounts. Discipline is not only for the difficult days and for the bad days, but also for the easy ones where you have great price action.

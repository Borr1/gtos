## 6. The daily-loss denominator — deterministic, not simulated

The bootstrap only ever draws days that exist in the series, so the largest single-day loss any path can take is `min(series) x risk`, on a day-start equity of at most `1 + target`. Where that is smaller than the 5 % allowance, **no path can breach under either denominator** and the choice is provably irrelevant — a stronger statement than `p_fail_daily = 0`.

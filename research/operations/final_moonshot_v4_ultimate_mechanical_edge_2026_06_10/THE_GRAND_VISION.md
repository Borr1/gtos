## Reframe

(market STATE -> forward outcome distribution, with odds + sample size + per-regime stability).
Strategies are HIGH-ODDS QUERIES against the map. Years of multi-regime data > any live window for
DISCOVERY; tick data lets us also calibrate EXECUTION, so live is confirmation, not a gate.

## I. Universal conditional-outcome map (the "90% engine")
- For any richly-described state S, compute empirical P(reach +xR before -yR | S) over years/instruments/TFs.
- HIGH ODDS COME FROM CONFLUENCE: independent conditions each nudge odds; stacked + co-occurring they
  multiply (58% x 62% x 60% x 64% intersection -> 75-85%). The edge is the INTERSECTION, not one pattern.
- The map judges STATES not systems -> "losing strategies" just average good+bad states; every loser is
  negative evidence on a cell, every winner positive. Nothing killed, everything compounds.

- Volume/auction profile (Market Profile/TPO) from M1/tick: value area, POC, HVN/LVN; price defends value,
  rejects voids -> maps where MMs are positioned. NOT BUILT YET.
- Absorption vs aggression (tick-volume microstructure): activity w/o progress = absorption (reversal fuel);
  activity w/ progress = aggression (continuation). Delta proxies.
- Institutional schedules: London/NY/4pm fixes, month/quarter-end rebalance, futures roll, expiry pins.
- Cross-instrument lead-lag / intermarket: DXY->FX, yields->equities, oil->CAD, gold<->real-yields, risk on/off.
  Trade the laggard. NOT BUILT YET — whole uncorrelated edge family.

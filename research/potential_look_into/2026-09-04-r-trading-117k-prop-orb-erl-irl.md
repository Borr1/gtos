## Post

About 3 months ago I made a post showing that I had received around **$95K in prop firm payouts** and broke down the two setups I was using. That post blew up way more than I expected so here's the update.

I'm now at **$117,397 in total payouts with $8,638 spent on evaluations and resets which puts me at roughly $108,759 net**.

I still mainly trade the **15 minute ORB + FVG** and **ERL → IRL**. ORB is my simple opening momentum setup.

ERL → IRL is what I use for the larger liquidity based moves and weekly swings.

What I have realized is that I don't need to trade both equally.

I track every setup separately and the data has made it pretty obvious that **ERL → IRL has been my strongest model recently**. So when I get A+ setup, that's where I'm willing to put more risk on.

Here's what six years of trading taught me that no course or YouTube video ever will. The market doesn't care about your analysis. It doesn't care how many confluences you stacked or how many hours you spent on your game plan. It either moves in your direction or it doesn't, and your only job is to make sure you survive the times it doesn't so you're still around for the times it does. That means small size, hard stops, and the ability to take a loss without it turning into three more. Every blown account in my career came from the same place, I was right about the direction but wrong about the timing, and instead of taking the loss I doubled down and also one of my biggest downfalls was trying to catch a falling knife.

If you want the full breakdown of both setups with chart examples, entry rules, stop placement, and risk management, I've posted detailed walkthroughs of the 15-min ORB and the ERL → IRL framework on here before and I'm happy to do updated versions. Drop a comment if that's useful and I'll put them together.

Most of these trades were copy traded across my funded accounts which is how the payouts scaled. More importantly the prop payouts eventually gave me enough capital to open my own cash account and I've been trading the exact same models there too.

I'm not pretending the road from $95K to $117K was straight up either. I've had drawdowns bad trades and periods where the smartest thing I could do was barely trade. The difference now is a losing week doesn't make me go searching for a new strategy and instead i spend time manually backtesting and even running full on automations to tweak the strats.

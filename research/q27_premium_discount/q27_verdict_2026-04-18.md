## Why the verdict is DEFER, not SHIP

1. **Frequency-adjusted expectancy is NEGATIVE under a gate.** Headline WR lift +4.27 pp
   looks good, but the gate drops trade count by ~24 %; at min_rr 1.5 the monthly R
   decreases (+14.7R → +12.7R). "Higher WR, fewer trades" is selection, not lift.
2. **Redundancy risk with existing OB-zone edge is plausible and untested.** Section §6's
   own mechanism hypothesis suggests the reverse signal may be a geometric restatement of
   "typical OB geometry wins; atypical OB geometry loses" — which the +17 pp OB-zone
   advantage already captures. No orthogonality check exists.
3. **XAUUSD (primary symbol) is flat (p=0.85).** The pooled signal is driven by GBPUSD
   and US30. Practical impact on the live system is smaller than the pooled stats
   imply.
4. **2026-only.** No OOS. Inverting a taught heuristic demands confirmation.
5. **Pre-challenge posture.** Tuesday 2026-04-21 start; any src/prompts/config change
   requires CEO approval + canary revalidation. Shadow-only is the only pre-challenge
   safe option, and even that carries a decision cost.

## Why the verdict is DEFER, not KILL

1. **Pre-registered and symmetric.** The report did the rigorous work: committed
   hypothesis, thresholds, decision rules BEFORE looking at outcomes. Pooled p=0.000184
   is ~27× below α. Both LONG and SHORT subsets show the same-sign gap. That is
   qualitatively different from post-hoc ICT overfits.
2. **The REVERSE signal has a plausible mechanism.** §6 gives a non-trivial structural
   reason why OB-retests cluster in "wrong-SMC-side" — the geometry of OB detection
   itself places the OB near the BOS-extreme. This is testable and would explain why
   ICT teaches backwards for this specific framework.
3. **Two independent impulse definitions converge.** BOS-anchored 3-bar pivot (primary)
   and production's last-pivot-pair definition (robustness check) both reject SMC with
   p < 0.001. Non-trivially robust.
4. **Cheap to shadow-log.** Zero-code-change route exists (read-only script consuming
   existing `pipeline_state/02_market_state.json` + trade index). 30+ OOS trades per
   symbol will give a definitive answer in ~6 months of live data.

Kill requires evidence AGAINST. The report gives evidence FOR a (reverse) effect in-sample.
What it doesn't give is OOS confirmation or orthogonality proof. DEFER is the appropriate
treatment.

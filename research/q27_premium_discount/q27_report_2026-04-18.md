## 1. PRE-REGISTRATION (committed before examining outcomes)

### 1.1 Impulse swing definition

The "impulse" is the BOS-forming leg, identified from H1 OHLC:

- **Pivots** detected with `SWING_WINDOW=1` (3-bar strict pivot) on H1 — matches A2_v2's cadence and therefore the dataset that produced the retests being classified.
- **Bullish BOS (LONG retest):**
  - `impulse_low` = price of the most recent confirmed swing LOW strictly before `bos_idx`.
  - `impulse_high` = max H1 high from that swing-low index through `bos_idx` inclusive (actual extreme reached during the impulse leg).
- **Bearish BOS (SHORT retest):** mirror definition; `impulse_high` = most recent prior pivot high price, `impulse_low` = min H1 low over the leg.
- If no prior pivot of the required side exists, the retest is **excluded** and counted in `no_prior_swing`.

### 1.2 Zone mapping (Fibonacci, price-location-based)

`fib_pct = (entry_price - impulse_low) / (impulse_high - impulse_low)` — clipped to [0, 1] for labelling; raw value is preserved in the CSV for diagnostics.

| `fib_pct` range | Zone label |
|---|---|
| [0.000, 0.382] | **discount** (lower third) |
| (0.382, 0.618) | **equilibrium** (middle) |
| [0.618, 1.000] | **premium** (upper third) |

### 1.3 Correct-side / wrong-side mapping (per task brief)

- **LONG** setups: correct-side = **discount**; wrong-side = **premium**. Equilibrium = neutral (excluded from binary test).
- **SHORT** setups: correct-side = **premium**; wrong-side = **discount**. Equilibrium = neutral (excluded from binary test).

### 1.4 Sample thresholds & tests

- n ≥ 30 per zone-stratum for inclusion in Bonferroni comparisons. Symbols below threshold on either side are marked `underpowered`.
- **Primary test:** 2-proportion z-test on pooled correct-side WR vs wrong-side WR.
- **Per-symbol tests:** same test per symbol, Bonferroni α / 5 = 0.01.
- **Effect-size threshold to call actionable:** ≥ 5 pp WR advantage for correct-side.
- **Decision:**
  - SIGNAL FOUND: pooled p < 0.05 AND ≥ 5 pp advantage AND consistent direction.
  - NULL: pooled p ≥ 0.05 OR |gap| < 5 pp.
  - UNDERPOWERED: < 3 symbols fully powered.

### 1.5 Outcome coding

`outcome_a = CONTINUED` ⇒ win = 1; `REVERSED` ⇒ win = 0; `UNRESOLVED` excluded.

## 6. Verdict

### **NULL** for the SMC-standard hypothesis; **SIGNAL FOUND in the reverse direction**

- The pre-registered hypothesis ("OB retests on the correct SMC side — discount-buy / premium-sell — outperform wrong-side retests by ≥ 5 pp WR") is **REJECTED**.
- The data points to the **opposite** effect: OB retests that land in the "wrong" SMC zone relative to trade direction outperform "correct"-zone retests by a pooled 15.6 pp (p = 0.000184; 2/5 symbols individually significant after Bonferroni α/5 = 0.01; 0/5 symbols show the SMC-predicted direction with significance).
- Equilibrium retests also outperform correct-side (77.4 % vs 63.4 %).

### Mechanism hypothesis (informational, not pre-registered)

OB bodies sit near the BOS-side extreme of the impulse leg because the OB is defined as the **last opposing candle before the BOS**, which by construction is closer to the extreme than to the pullback origin. This means:

- For LONG (bullish BOS), the OB body is typically in the upper portion of the low→high impulse → LONG retests naturally land in "premium".
- For SHORT, mirror → SHORT retests naturally land in "discount".

The small subset of retests in the "correct" SMC zone (LONG in discount / SHORT in premium) are the cases where the OB is positioned atypically far from the BOS extreme — these are precisely the **low-conviction, weak-impulse setups** that underperform on base rates. The SMC heuristic happens to be backwards for the specific geometry of this system's OB-retest framework.

This mechanism story is a **hypothesis**, not a proven cause. It is logged for future investigation but does NOT drive any recommendation below.

## 7. Shadow-gate proposal

### 7.1 Direction of the proposed gate

**Invert the SMC convention** (if a gate is shipped):

- Block / de-weight LONG retests whose entry falls in the **discount** half of the BOS-anchored impulse.
- Block / de-weight SHORT retests whose entry falls in the **premium** half of the BOS-anchored impulse.

Effect on frequency: would reject 172/707 = **24.3 %** of otherwise-eligible OB-retest candidates. This is material — ~4 trades/month of the ~17 expected total.

### 7.2 Effect-size guidance for gate sizing

If the reverse gate is implemented (block correct-SMC-side retests):

- Pooled expectancy lift: correct-side is a subset that drags WR from 74.7 % (all included) to 78.95 % (wrong-side + equilibrium only) — **+4.3 pp WR**.
- Most of the signal concentrates in **GBPUSD and US30_cash**. XAUUSD shows no zone signal in either direction.

### 7.3 Recommendation

**SHIP AS SHADOW-LOG FIRST, NOT AS A LIVE GATE.**

Reasons:
1. A 2026-only window (Jan 2 → Apr 17) is the entire sample. The finding has not been tested out-of-sample on 2024 / 2025 H1 data. Possible that 2026 regime is non-representative.
2. The signal is heterogeneous across symbols. XAUUSD (our primary) shows +1.9 pp in the SMC-predicted direction and p = 0.85; the reverse signal is driven by GBPUSD and US30.
3. Inverting a widely-taught SMC heuristic is a claim that deserves cold review before production impact. Shadow logging gives 30+ real trades of out-of-sample confirmation before any gate activation.
4. The pre-registered effect-size threshold (≥ 5 pp) is cleared pooled, but XAUUSD alone does not clear it.

**Concrete proposal (for CEO decision; not an instruction):**

Add a shadow-only classifier that labels each live CANDIDATE's entry with `pd_zone ∈ {discount, equilibrium, premium}` computed from the same H1 pivots the production MSO uses. Append to `shadow_logs/pd_zone_log.csv`. After 30+ trades per symbol, re-test pooled and per-symbol. Promote to live gate only if the reverse signal holds out-of-sample and pooled gap remains ≥ 5 pp.

Zero-code-change alternative: classifier can be a read-only script consuming existing `pipeline_state/02_market_state.json` (which already contains `premium_discount`), joined with the shadow-log trade outcomes.

## 8. Methodology limitations

1. **BOS-anchored impulse is one of several defensible definitions.** Production `calculate_premium_discount` uses "most recent swing high/low" irrespective of BOS. An alternative analysis using that definition (with 5-bar H1 pivots matching `swing_detection_min_bars.H1 = 2`) was run as a robustness check; the pooled direction and significance were consistent (gap −15.44 pp, p = 0.0004), but ~29 % of entries fell outside [impulse_low, impulse_high] because 5-bar pivots miss the BOS-forming low/high pair that define the relevant impulse. The BOS-anchored 3-bar pivot methodology used in the primary analysis keeps 92.5 % of entries inside the impulse range.
2. **Retest dataset is 2026-only.** A2_v2 intentionally scoped to 2026-01-01 → 2026-04-17. No 2024/2025 generalisation in this report. Handoff to future work: re-run on `outputs/historical/combined_retests.csv` (121 retests, 2024–2025) as a first-pass OOS check — current n per symbol is 19–33 there, so Bonferroni-5 is under-powered per symbol but pooled is plausibly useful.
3. **UNRESOLVED handling.** 19 retests (2.6 %) excluded. Handled as "not a loss, not a win"; their inclusion as wins/losses or as 0.5 does not change the qualitative result (the effect is ≥ 13 pp in both directions within a 48-M15-candle horizon classifier).
4. **Zone classifier does not align with what the live AI sees.** The live Sonnet 4.6 T7 prompt does not receive explicit `pd_zone` for the BOS-anchored impulse; it gets production's `calculate_premium_discount` output (last pivot pair). If a gate is ever activated, it must be a pre-AI rule using the BOS-anchored impulse, not a post-hoc filter on the existing MSO field.
5. **No out-of-sample validation within this report.** The 2026 window is both the discovery set and the test set. This is a hypothesis-generating exercise, not a confirmatory test.

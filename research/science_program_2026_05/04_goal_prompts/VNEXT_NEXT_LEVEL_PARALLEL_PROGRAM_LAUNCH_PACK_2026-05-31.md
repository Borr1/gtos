## Shared Execution Standard

Every prompt operationalizes `.context/00_core/goal_session_research_discipline.md` and `.context/00_core/research_operating_doctrine.md` as active instructions, not background. Each lane must regenerate `.context/LIVE_STATE.md`, reread current vNext context, and do not rely on chat memory.

There is no arbitrary top-N, top 3/5/10, representative-only, or number-limited cutoff for questions, ambiguities, source roots, symbols, sessions, branches, failure families, opportunities, blockers, or implementation paths. Ranked summaries are allowed as views after a full ledger preserves all material rows.

Full same-evidence-class pursuit is required. Literal impossibility means exactly that every executable read, export, search, parser, repair, proxy, ablation, metric, audit, and review action available from the approved source class has been attempted or proven inapplicable with row-level source proof.

Research and local implementation work have no conservative brake inside their evidence class. External action boundary for this launch pack: live trading broker operation, paid API/vendor spending, credential changes, and hidden production-change deployment are outside the task. Reading broker/account/order/history/deal/position evidence is required wherever it informs truth. Prompt/config/risk/execution/safety/canary/selector code is in scope for inspection, tests, and local packages when a lane owns that work.

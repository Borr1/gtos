## Route-Selection Rules

Do not pick the biggest family blindly. Family size is useful, but rank must balance:

- source safety and as-of clarity,
- unique denominator quality,
- duplicate/concentration risk,
- compact artifact coverage versus aggregate count confidence,
- unresolved/same-bar ambiguity burden,
- whether the family tests a real GTOS-relevant mechanism,
- whether strong adversarial baselines exist,
- whether the downstream lane can remain no-API and source-safe,
- whether the downstream lane can produce interpretable failure anatomy if negative,
- whether the route is broad enough to avoid one-symbol/one-timeframe/one-session boxing,
- whether the route creates reusable evidence for later sealed historical validation.

Do not let the current GTOS edge box the synthesis. `ob_retest` is important, but the G0 route must seriously evaluate adjacent and orthogonal families, especially liquidity stop-run context, session/KZ sweep, FVG fill, opening-drive lifecycle, range-compression breakout, momentum continuation, and mean reversion. Baseline families are not throwaways; they are adversarial controls that can kill fake lift and must be preserved in downstream design.

Do not treat path-label status proportions as wins, losses, expectancy, or edge proof. In this G0 route, path labels may be used only as structural/path-behavior evidence for route selection, ambiguity assessment, baseline design, and failure-anatomy planning. If a downstream prompt will score any result-like outcome, that must be a separate quarantined discovery-result route with its own frozen denominator, duplicate policy, baseline controls, and mandatory post-result G12 audit.

Do not let the 120,000-row compact artifact cap distort ranking. Compact rows are valid for schema, row examples, sample-level cross-checks, and bounded sanity checks. Full-stream family counts, source-progress counters, accepted summaries, builder aggregate logic, and LFS materialized artifacts must drive full-population reasoning. If compact rows are used to estimate a cross-tab that is not available in aggregate ledgers, label it as compact-sample-only unless the route builds a source-safe aggregate.

Do not let downstream prompt creation stop at the first acceptable route. The G0 route must state why rank 1 beats ranks 2-4, whether any routes should run in parallel, and what exact evidence-class gate follows each selected route. If multiple parallel prompts are emitted, each must have disjoint write scope and no dependency on another unfinished route.

Do not stop because there are many rows. Use the accepted summaries, compact LFS artifacts, source-progress ledgers, builder aggregate logic, and bounded reruns or parsers where feasible. If a needed aggregate is missing and can be built inside this evidence class, build it. If it would require result scoring, validation, AI/API, tick/spread contract work, depth parser work, or production intent source-state, route it exactly instead of pretending it is answered.

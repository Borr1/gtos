## Domain Synthesis

Momentum and mean reversion should be modeled as horizon-conditioned behavior rather than as permanent opposites. The local GTOS evidence points to a primitive in which an impulse creates a displaced zone, price reverts into that pre-cascade area, and continuation then resumes if the structural impulse remains intact. This is not a promotion claim; it is a research hypothesis constrained by duplicate policy, source contracts, and G0's same-dataset validation rules.

The strongest current local evidence is the Test A rerun: mechanical OB retest continuation remained stronger than dumb retrace baselines in the historical rerun, but the earlier headline relative OB advantage failed DSR and AI selection did not add measurable value in that population. Therefore the surviving primitive is "zone-conditioned continuation may exist"; the killed claim is "the prior headline relative lift is validation-safe."

The dumb momentum baseline complicates the story. On the same XAU Jan-Apr 2026 window, a simple 80 percent retrace model looked competitive with GTOS F3 on a small sample, while the April collapse was not reproduced by the dumb baseline. This makes the near-term research question sharper: whether the useful part is structural zone precision, a generic continuation/retrace effect, AI/filter selection, or a regime interaction between them.

Continuation/no-retrace is promising but source-blocked. The current audit found many candidates whose later path reached target area without a limit touch, but only a tiny countable primary set and no exact decision-entry price or ordered M1/tick path sufficient for R scoring. These rows remain lifecycle context only.

Opening range and session-drive continuation should be handled asymmetrically. Gold Asian range sweeps are already recorded as overwhelmingly continuation-like, so direct sweep-fade logic is killed. A separate opening-drive continuation experiment can still be preregistered if it uses decision-time session levels and explicitly excludes killed session-sweep reversal framing.

Reversal exhaustion remains a separate primitive. The dumb-baseline report showed FVG-in-impulse without OB constraint inverted in the sample, suggesting some displacement features may mark exhaustion rather than continuation. Literature on slow momentum with fast reversion and changepoint detection supports testing a state transition layer, but not using that literature as validation.

Stat-arb analogues are useful conceptually but blocked operationally. Stop-cascade reversion to a structural pivot is analogous to residual reversion around an equilibrium, and gold/silver or correlated-instrument residuals are plausible research features. They require strict source contracts, synchronized as-of timestamps, cost controls, and validation-safe unseen cohorts before any production claim.

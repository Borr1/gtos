## Mechanisms Worth Finding

1. Crowded SMC/ICT attention decay: public attention to order-block/FVG/ICT-like language may make the setup crowded enough to reduce continuation quality or compress winners.
2. Retail herding at visible levels: retail positioning or search attention may cluster around simple direction narratives, creating contrarian or reversal stress at GTOS POIs.
3. Predatory forced-flow cascades: when constrained traders must reduce exposure, strategic traders can trade in the same direction first and exploit forced liquidation before mean reversion.
4. Adaptive-market ecology: the same GTOS primitive can move between efficient and inefficient states as competitor attention, volatility, and regime change.
5. News and attention herding: scheduled macro/news windows may attract synchronized attention, widen ambiguity, and create nonstationary response paths.
6. AI narrative anchoring: LLM or prompt framing can over-weight SMC narrative coherence relative to mechanical baselines, especially when the setup is ambiguous.

## Counter-Evidence And Decay Modes

Counter-evidence reviewed:

- Institutional herding literature contains null/weak evidence at some frequencies and markets; any G5 herding row must expect regime and frequency sensitivity.
- Christie and Huang show that dispersion may rise in stress, inconsistent with simple market-wide herding. GTOS tests must use source-specific retail/proxy cohorts rather than assume all stress is herd behavior.
- Kurov, Wolfe, and Gilbert report disappearance of the pre-FOMC drift after 2015, showing that attention/news anomalies can decay.
- Local dumb-baseline evidence warns that SMC/AI framing may not add much over mechanical momentum, so AI-narrative hypotheses must include mechanical comparators.

Known decay modes:

- Public adoption/crowding turns edge into adverse selection.
- Source proxies are too coarse for XAUUSD intraday decisions.
- Broker sentiment or search interest measures attention, not actual executable flow.
- Calendar or news source staleness introduces false non-event labels.
- LLM reasoning changes with model/version/prompt and cannot be mixed across cohorts without version locks.

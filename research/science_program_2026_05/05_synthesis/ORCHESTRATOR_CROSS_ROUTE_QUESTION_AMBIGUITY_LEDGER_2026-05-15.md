## Update Rule

Every route should either close its owned questions with disk artifact references or append newly discovered questions. Do not close a question from a pasted closeout alone.

No route may cap questions, ambiguities, possibilities, open doors, opportunities, blockers, source roots, branches, routes, or failure families at an arbitrary number such as top 3, top 5, or top 10. Preserve every material question the data exposes until each is answered, killed with data, repaired, proven impossible, or routed with exact owner/access/source/capture requirements.

The JSON ledger carries the closure schema. Before any question can become closed, the route must record closure artifacts, child question ids, status history, closing commit, answer summary, and remaining risk.

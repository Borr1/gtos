## Highest-Transfer Ideas

These are the most immediately translatable ideas, with source anchors. The complete, uncapped ledgers are `SOURCE_LEDGER_2026-05-15.jsonl` and `HYPOTHESIS_LEDGER_2026-05-15.jsonl`.

1. OFI around entry touch

   Cont, Kukanov, and Stoikov show a robust relation between short-horizon price changes and order-flow imbalance at the best queues (`S001`, https://arxiv.org/abs/1011.6402). GTOS already has candidate timestamps and some tick/orderflow capture lanes. Translate into `ofi_pre_touch_z`, adverse queue depletion, and OFI slope features for OB/FVG/breaker candidates.

2. Footprint/CVD as a boundary-quality measure

   Sierra Numbers Bars and TradingView CVD docs identify practical bid/ask delta, CVD, POC-volume, and volume-speed fields (`S003`, `S006`). Translate into absorption/divergence descriptors at OB/FVG/breaker levels. This is a natural mechanical substitute for vague discretionary "absorption" language.

3. Volume-profile target and avoid-friction logic

   Sierra and thinkorswim docs give computable POC, VAH/VAL, and profile period/tick-binning constraints (`S004`, `S005`). Translate into profile-node confluence, value-area rejection, LVN traversal, and HVN/POC target-friction tests.

4. LBMA and WMR fix windows as deterministic event regimes

   LBMA/ICE define gold auction timing and details (`S007`, `S008`), while FCA/Evans/IOSCO support the 4pm FX fix as a distinct market-behavior window (`S009`, `S010`, `S011`). Translate into DST-aware event calendars and test signal versus execution friction around benchmark windows.

5. Duration/hazard features for candidate timing and pending-limit lifecycle

   Engle and Russell's ACD model supports event-duration thinking (`S012`). Translate into time-since-candidate, candidate clustering, price-change duration, and pending-limit fill survival models.

6. Futures/proxy confirmation and cross-market liquidity propagation

   Lead-lag and OFR cross-asset orderflow sources (`S015`, `S016`) plus gold futures price-discovery evidence (`S017`) support serious proxy testing. Translate into GC/XAUUSD, SI/XAGUSD, NQ/NAS100, YM/US30, 6J/USDJPY, and 6B/GBPUSD confirmation/veto features, with official CME normalization (`S018`, `S019`).

7. Execution realism as a strategy selector

   Fill uncertainty and market-impact papers (`S020`, `S021`) translate into pending-limit survival, marketable-limit counterfactuals, and no-arbitrage cost sanity checks. This can convert "good signal but unfillable" into useful routing intelligence.

8. Public slow-regime data

   CFTC COT and Fed H10 data are official/free (`S022` to `S025`). Translate into as-of weekly/daily regime filters for metals/FX, with release-lag and revision controls.

9. Validation rails before idea ranking

   PBO and DSR sources (`S026`, `S027`) are mandatory for the moonshot's many-idea search. Translate into a trial-count/debt ledger and purged/embargoed validation harness before any "best strategy" interpretation.

10. Causal signal-processing challengers

   Kalman filtering, Kalman-HMM, CUSUM, and wavelet sources (`S028` to `S032`) translate into causal hidden-state, proxy-basis, change-point, and multiscale impulse descriptors. These must be compared against simple baselines and guarded against lookahead leakage.

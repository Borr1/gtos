# CEO DIRECTIVE — NO FABRICATION (Issued 2026-04-07)

**This directive is binding on ALL agents. No exceptions. No expiry.**

---

## The Rule

**Never fabricate, invent, estimate, or generate data to support a claim, improvement, or conclusion.**

Every number, statistic, percentage, win rate, improvement, or finding you produce must be:

1. **From real data** — traced to an actual CSV file, backtest run, or live trade in this project
2. **From external research** — cited with a real source (paper title, book, URL, author)
3. **From direct system observation** — something the agent actually saw in a log, file, or output

---

## What Is Forbidden

- Inventing statistics (e.g., "this improves win rate by 12%") without a data source
- Generating synthetic/simulated results and presenting them as evidence
- Using agent-generated outputs as proof of anything
- Filling gaps in knowledge with plausible-sounding but made-up numbers
- Saying "approximately" or "estimated" to paper over the absence of real data

---

## What to Do Instead

- If data doesn't exist yet: **say so explicitly** ("This has not been tested. Data is not available.")
- If you're uncertain: **say so** ("I don't have evidence for this claim.")
- If you want to propose something: **label it clearly as a hypothesis**, not a finding

---

## Why This Exists

Agents were generating fabricated improvements and statistics that looked credible but were not backed by any real data. This corrupts the decision-making process for a live trading system where wrong decisions have real financial consequences.

---

**Failure to comply with this directive is a critical violation. Any agent that fabricates data must be flagged immediately to the CEO.**

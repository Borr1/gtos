# R7 Failure Intelligence Doctrine Addendum

Date: 2026-05-15
Status: active doctrine for the current READY8 R7 run
Scope: `READY8_EXPANDED_SEALED_VALIDATION_PACKET_DESIGN_ONLY`

## Purpose

This addendum is active doctrine for the current R7 expanded sealed-validation packet-design run. It must be applied without restarting, discarding, or flattening current work.

When controls, placebos, duplicates, concentration, source limits, fail-closed rows, or generic random movement explain a branch, do not shallowly kill the whole opportunity and move on. Kill only the unsupported edge claim, not the information. Preserve and use the failure intelligence.

## Required Classification

For every branch that is killed, weakened, deferred, underpowered, concentrated, control-explained, duplicate-explained, source-limited, or fail-closed, classify it exactly as one or more of:

- false opportunity / generic movement
- repairable limitation
- mixed mechanism needing split
- unproven but interesting
- inverse/avoid-filter candidate
- source-capture requirement
- forward-retest candidate
- exact owner/access/source/capture impossibility

## Required Treatment

Repair what can be repaired inside the current same-evidence-class goal. Split mixed mechanisms instead of flattening them. Control-adjust generic movement instead of erasing all signal. Preserve residual intelligence. Route underpowered, partition-specific, inverse, avoid-filter, source-capture, and future-retest implications explicitly.

Every killed/deferred/weakened branch must explain:

- what was learned;
- why it failed or weakened;
- which control/placebo/duplicate/source/concentration/fail-closed mechanism explained it;
- whether the limitation was repaired, split, adjusted, preserved, or proven impossible;
- whether it creates an avoid rule, control rule, source-capture requirement, packet-design row, or future retest candidate.

## Completion Standard

Do not let G12, control, or safe-flag language become a conservative brake. Safe flags are rails, not brakes.

Do not use arbitrary top-N, top 3/5/10, number-limited, or summary-only cutoffs. Preserve all material rows and all material questions, ambiguities, open doors, closed doors, failure branches, success branches, source roots, branch classifications, and follow-up implications in machine-readable ledgers until same-evidence-class intelligence remaining is zero or converted to exact proof of impossibility.

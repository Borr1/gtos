# Observer guide (friends)

1. Install MetaTrader 5 on your phone (App Store / Play Store).
2. Open the **Free Trial / demo** login Borhen sends you (not a paid challenge).
3. Watch positions appear when the system is live (fixed micro lots, comment `fleet:{ticket}`). You are viewing Challenge login **0** mirrored onto that demo — you do not trade the Challenge account.
4. Send feedback with: ticket number + one of:

   `too early` / `too late` / `ok timing` / `wrong session` / `wrong symbol` / `stop too tight` / `stop too wide` / `should have flat` / `should have held` / `unclear`

   One-line note optional.
5. You do not configure servers, EAs, or risk. Viewing + feedback only.

Chair (redacted_account) never places on the Challenge book from this path. A separate demo worker may open the mirrored micro-lot on your trial login only. If nothing is open, that is normal — the Challenge book is sparse. Telegram feedback is notes, not orders.

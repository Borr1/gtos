# M5 Entry Refinement — Comprehensive Validation Backtest

**Date:** 2026-04-03
**Data:** 526 mechanical trades + 14 AI trades, M5 data from 2024-12-20 to 2026-04-02
**Cost:** $0.00 (pure computation on existing data)
**Methods:** Mechanical M5 swing detection, SL floor sweep (7 levels × 3 entries × 3 slippage), split validation

---

## Executive Summary

1. **$10 SL floor is the optimal deployment parameter.** The feasibility test's $3-5 stops were too tight for market entry. At $10, the 14 AI trades produce +11.35R vs +4.59R baseline — a 147% improvement with only 1 extra stop-out.

2. **Market entry (at M15 confirmation) is the correct method.** Limit entries at M5 OB prices only fill 29% of the time. Market entry captures every trade and still benefits from the tighter TP target that M5 SL sizing enables.

3. **The realistic R improvement is +0.48R per trade** (from +0.33 to +0.81 average), not the +0.82R from the feasibility test. The difference: feasibility assumed perfect limit fills at M5 OB prices with $3 stops. Reality requires market entry with $10 floor stops.

4. **Results hold under slippage stress testing.** At $10 floor with $0.30 normal slippage, the mechanical validation still shows +0.443 avg R across 526 trades. The strategy doesn't collapse under execution friction.

5. **Deploy: M5 refinement with $10 SL floor, market entry, HIGH+MEDIUM quality gate.** Estimated improvement: +$480/trade at $1K risk. One extra Sonnet call per trade (~$0.013).

| Method | Fill Rate | Avg R | Total R | Verdict |
|--------|-----------|-------|---------|---------|
| **Market** | **100%** | **+0.81** | **+11.35** | **Best overall** |
| Limit | 29% (4/14) | +0.51 | +2.02 | Too few fills |
| Hybrid | 100% | +0.66 | +9.24 | Second best |

**Market entry wins decisively** because it captures every trade. Limit orders at M5 OB prices only fill 4 out of 14 times — price often doesn't pull back to the OB zone before continuing in the trade direction.

The hybrid approach (try limit for 20 min, then market fallback) underperforms pure market because the 20-minute delay means some trades run away before the market fill. Market entry at M15 confirmation is the simplest and best approach.

## Section 5: Deployment Recommendation

### The Configuration

```
M5 REFINEMENT CONFIGURATION:
  SL floor: $10.00
  Entry method: Market (at M15 confirmation candle close)
  Slippage buffer: $0.30 added to SL ($10.30 effective minimum)
  Max lots: 1.0 per $1K risk (at $10 SL, this is automatic)
  Quality gate: HIGH + MEDIUM only (from M5 AI analysis)
  Fallback: M15 SL when M5 quality = LOW or NO_REFINEMENT
  TP: 1.5 × floored SL distance = $15 at minimum floor

  Validated on: 526 mechanical trades + 14 AI trades
  Stable under stress slippage: YES
  Split-validated: YES (both halves positive)
```

### Expected Performance

| Avg R per trade | +0.33 | +0.81 | **+0.48R** |
| TP Hit Rate | 14% | 64% | **+50 points** |

| Dollar P&L per trade ($1K risk) | $330 | **$810** | **+$480** |

### Pipeline Changes Needed

1. **M5 data pull:** Add to data_ingestion — pull M5 candles for current day at KZ start
2. **M5 refinement call:** After M15 entry confirmation, call Sonnet with 36 M5 candles (~$0.013, ~5 sec)
3. **SL floor enforcement:** `actual_sl_dist = max(ai_m5_sl_dist, 10.0)`
4. **TP computation:** `tp = entry + 1.5 × actual_sl_dist` (for longs)
5. **Position sizing:** `lots = min(risk_dollars / (actual_sl_dist × 100), 5.0)`
6. **Fallback:** If M5 quality = LOW or decision = NO_REFINEMENT, use M15 SL as before

### Why Not $3 or $5?

The feasibility test showed $3 stops working perfectly. What changed?

**Entry price difference.** The feasibility test used the AI's M5 OB entry price (a limit order deep in the OB zone). This validation test uses market entry at M15 close price. The M15 close is typically $5-12 further from the M5 swing point than the M5 OB entry.

With market entry at M15 price + $3 SL, the stop is only $3 from M15 entry — but the M5 swing point the stop is based on might be $7 below the M5 entry. Since we're entering at M15 price (not M5), the $3 SL puts us $4 above the actual structural level. Result: stops hit on normal pullbacks.

The $10 floor ensures the stop is always at or below the structural level even when entering at M15 price.

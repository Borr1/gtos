# M5 Entry Refinement — Feasibility Test Results

**Date:** 2026-04-03
**Data:** 14 of 18 Phase 1 trades with M5 coverage (2025-02-18 to 2026-01-27)
**API Cost:** $0.18 total (14 Sonnet calls)
**Method:** AI reads 36 M5 candles before entry → suggests refined entry/SL → simulate 4 approaches on M5 data

---

## Executive Summary

1. **The AI finds valid M5 structure on 93% of trades (13/14).** It identifies M5 order blocks on every single refined trade, and rates 9/13 as HIGH quality. The M5 timeframe is rich with usable structure.

2. **Approach A (M5 entry + M5 SL) is the clear winner: +16.1R total vs +4.6R baseline — a 3.5x improvement.** TP hit rate jumps from 14% to 79% because the tighter SL ($5.39 avg vs $35.53) produces achievable dollar targets.

3. **ZERO additional stop-outs in Approach A.** Despite reducing SL by 85%, not a single trade that survived on M15 SL got stopped out on M5 SL. This is the critical finding — the M5 structural stops are VALID.

4. **Approach B (M5 entry + M15 SL) is the guaranteed-safe improvement: +6.7R vs +4.6R (+46%).** Same SL as baseline, just better entry prices. Cannot produce more stop-outs by definition.

5. **GO for M5 integration.** Approach A should be the primary strategy. Approach B serves as the fallback when no M5 structure exists. Estimated improvement: +0.82R per trade ($820 per $1K risk).

### 2.3 The Key Insight: Why Approach A Wins

The average M15 SL distance is $35.53. At 1.5R TP, the dollar target is $53.30.

The average M5 SL distance is $5.39. At 1.5R TP, the dollar target is **$8.09**.

Gold can move $8 easily within a KZ session. Gold moving $53 within a single session is unusual.

The M5 refinement doesn't change the DIRECTION of the trade — it changes the SCALE of the bet. The trade direction is already validated on M15/H1/H4/D1. M5 just finds a tighter structural reference point.

### 2.4 Zero Additional Stop-Outs

This is the most surprising and important finding. Despite reducing the stop loss by 85% (from $35.53 to $5.39 on average), Approach A produced **ZERO additional stop-outs** compared to baseline.

Why? Because the AI is finding genuine M5 swing points — structural levels that hold. When the M5 structure confirms (BOS or higher-low), the retracement typically doesn't violate that structure. The M5 stop is placed at the structural invalidation level, which turns out to be valid.

Trade #7 (2025-09-23) is particularly telling: the baseline got stopped out at -0.231R, but Approach A hit TP at +1.500R. The M5 entry was $6.3 better than M15, and the M5 SL at $3.24 held because the M5 structure was valid.

## Section 5: Implementation Recommendation

### GO — Deploy Approach A as Primary Strategy

**Pipeline Changes:**

1. **Data Ingestion:** Add M5 data pull to the data_ingestion pipeline. M5 data for the current day should be pulled at the start of each KZ (07:00 UTC for London, 13:00 UTC for NY).

2. **Second API Call:** After the M15 entry is confirmed, immediately call Sonnet with the M5 refinement prompt. This adds ~5 seconds and ~$0.013 per trade.

3. **Execution:**
   - If decision = REFINED and quality ≥ MEDIUM:
     - Use M5 entry price as limit order
     - Use M5 SL as stop loss
     - TP = M5_entry + 1.5 × M5_SL_distance (LONG)
   - If decision = NO_REFINEMENT or quality = LOW:
     - Fall back to M15 entry and M15 SL (Approach B-style)

4. **Position Sizing Impact:** With $5.39 avg SL instead of $35.53, position size per $1K risk increases 6.6x. This means the DOLLAR profit per trade scales dramatically. A +1.5R trade with $5 SL and $1K risk = $1,500 profit. The same +1.5R with $35 SL = $1,500 profit (same R, same dollar risk), but the ENTRY PRICE IMPROVEMENT means more trades reach +1.5R.

### Risk Mitigation

1. **Small sample:** 14 trades is enough to show direction but not statistical significance. Run 50+ additional backtests before going live with full position sizing.

2. **M5 SL too tight?** Monitor the first 10 live trades. If >2 get stopped out at M5 SL that would have survived at M15 SL, add a $3-5 buffer (move toward Approach C).

3. **Slippage:** M5 entries are limit orders in the OB zone. In live trading, the fill might be $0.50-1.00 worse than the theoretical entry. This is still much better than M15 entry.

4. **The AI might hallucinate structure:** 2/14 trades had the AI suggest entries outside the valid OB zone (automatically corrected). Add validation constraints to the pipeline.

## THESIS
SPINE optimises the cost of an honest answer, because that cost is currently the program's governor: an economic question costs 16.5 h of machine time (no sub-window — `b7_5_post_acceleration_runner.py:832` hardcodes bounded mode off [VERIFIED]), ~8 GB of RAM (one arm at a time, and the ≤3 GB gate is measured unreachable through the source layer — B84/B85 [MEASURED]), and 15.75 GB of JSON per arm of which 92 % is never read by any decision (60.6 % of CPU proves, 8.3 % decides — FINAL_INDEPENDENT_AUDIT.md:67-73,184 [MEASURED]). The architecture inverts the proof economics rather than shrinking the proof: the hot loop emits only a minimal typed event spine; every evidentiary artifact — ledgers, scorecards, attribution, divergence matrices, learning frames — becomes a deterministic offline projection of spine + content-addressed inputs; fidelity is guaranteed by delegation to live code (Session H's proven pattern: zero disagreements over 617 unit-bearing live cycles [MEASURED B92]) and by differential parity (Session D's harness), not by emission volume. This wins on the charter's own terms because every open activation decision — OD-1's premise (G4: are the five silent core sleeves broken or just slow?), the composition-vs-measured-costs question (F38/F39: the only positive validation in the program charged zero commission), and the B7.5 read-out — is blocked on evidence quality, not on more machinery; and the same rebuild that answers them cheaply is the charter's replay-at-scale → feature-store → learning-loop substrate. Activation-first is defended literally: the first four steps each terminate in sealed economics or an owner decision packet on the activation path, and the evidence rebuild is financed by those wins, not scheduled ahead of them.

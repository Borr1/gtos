## THESIS
Every backward measurement GTOS ever trusted about its live surface failed on a term nobody modelled — the validation charged zero commission at five independent sites (F38), the authorizing MC credited tick erosion with the wrong sign (F39), the sealed replay measured a stack that was ordered off while the declared-live book went unmeasured, both audits had the broker clock wrong, and the packet field that would have caught the cost story was declared and NULL on all 99,112 rows (F36) — while every decisive economic truth the program now owns (commission structure per symbol to four decimals, the JPY edge dead at p=0.00016, stops filling clean at −1.0036 R, slippage small, the derisk governor working) came from fourteen days of forward evidence that twelve years of backtest could not see. FORWARD COURT therefore inverts the evidence architecture: the live/forward channel becomes the primary measurement instrument (capture repaired, both accounts, every decision cycle), the book is replayed only against captured live data through the already-validated `SleeveBookPolicy` (zero disagreements over 617 unit-bearing cycles because it delegates to the production admission function itself), historical replay is demoted to hypothesis generation, and activation is re-framed as pre-registered, bounded, owner-authorized *measurement* whose upside is the payout. It wins because it splits evidence by convergence rate — cost and safety converge in days forward and gate activation; per-sleeve edge converges in years and is handled by composition, repaired priors, and bounded canary risk instead of by waiting — and because it makes the F36 failure mode (silently capturing nothing where it matters) structurally impossible before the first real-money decision, not after.

## COMPONENTS
## The court metaphor, stated once

Every economic question in this architecture is settled in one place: a **Forward Court session** — a deterministic adjudication of captured live data. Historical replay may *propose* (hypotheses, priors, candidate sleeves); only forward evidence *disposes*. Session F's verdict `REPLAY_R_IS_NOT_A_LIVE_CLAIM` (58 rows, 43 blocking) is adopted as a permanent invariant, not a finding to be fixed — the strongest row is unfixable by construction: 47 of 111 live signals fired on only one account, a divergence "no replay analogue... can measure at any window length" [VERIFIED `IMPLEMENTATION_STATE.md:2267,2272-2275`].

## RISKS
1. **The internal contradiction, named.** Activation still leans on the repaired CYCLE62 prior — precisely the evidence class this angle demotes. Forward-only can measure costs and safety fast, but the *edge* at canary time is a backward claim with commission patched in. If the prior is wrong in some further unmodelled way (the F38-shaped hole this architecture cannot prove absent), the canary measures a negative at real cost. The architecture bounds that cost and maximises what each lost R teaches; it cannot make the prior true.
2. **The calendar confession bites hardest exactly where conviction is highest.** If G4 resolves as "natural low frequency", then `metals_core` (conf 1.00, the registry's deepest anchor) accrues forward evidence slowest of all — the bench will chronically say INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE for the sleeves the book most depends on, and ranking falls back to cluster/cost/prior: demoted evidence again. The 2.00% dial is only expressible through that sleeve (`admission.py:1038-1040`), so the dial's ceiling stays untested for months.
3. **14 days is one regime.** The decisive cost findings are one fortnight, two brokers, one volatility state. Commission is contractual and stable-ish; but a term the broker introduces later that FEP-2 doesn't declare is F36/F38 all over again. Continuous broker-truth reconciliation in every FVR detects such a hole after the fact, not before.
4. **Shadow never tests fills.** Would-trade records don't experience queue position, requotes, or the book's own impact. Slippage measured small (+0.0132 R) at this size; nothing says it stays small at a higher dial. And the 42% single-account divergence is *captured*, not *explained* — if it is tick-arrival nondeterminism, court-vs-live disagreement rows may be chronically noisy, and the triage burden could quietly become the new proof machinery (the 60.6% failure mode reborn in the court).
5. **S4 is a single risky act on a running, funded, connected host.** A capture regression darkens the primary instrument; a deployment error on the one-boolean-brake host is the nightmare scenario. Mitigated by staging (capture first, token second) and by the token's fail-closed design — at the price of two owner actions instead of one.
6. **Two-machine, manually-exported truth.** Courts on the laptop, capture on the VPS; the 07-02 ledger deaths (V3) proved exhaust dies silently. A missed export week is a hole in the primary evidence stream. The architecture needs an export cadence with an alarm on silence, and that is unbuilt.
7. **The owner may reject the epistemology.** If Borhen's bar is "prove positive edge forward before real money", this architecture answers that the bar is unmeetable in reasonable time by any architecture — honest, but schedule-hostile, and it cancelled the historical campaign that the previous plan treated as the road to that proof. If he rejects the reframing, the stopped April/May/March campaign must restart, and this architecture wasted the cancellation.

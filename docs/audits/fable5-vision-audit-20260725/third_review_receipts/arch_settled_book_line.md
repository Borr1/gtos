## THESIS
GTOS today holds exactly one positively-validated policy family — the W7 sleeve book, whose 2014-2026 locked validation is corrupted by a cost model that charges zero commission at five verified sites (F38) and credited tick erosion with the wrong sign (F39) — and one family with uniformly negative evidence everywhere it has been measured (the broad V4 stack: four negative sealed cells, −0.25 R/fill live, 1W/20L holdout). Session E dissolved the W7 live fortnight as evidence against the book (11.5 % of validated confidence weight traded; 76.4 % of the loss came from an unvalidated candidate book), Session H made the book's decision side exactly replayable by delegation to the live code, the broker-true costs are already measured per symbol, and the per-trade validation ledgers sit in-tree with sleeve, symbol, and R on every row. This architecture therefore optimises for the shortest honest path to first payout by re-costing the only positive evidence into a survivor book, settling the one question (G4: why were the five highest-confidence sleeves silent?) that separates that book from an activation claim, and confirming it on the forward shadow stream the VPS is already accumulating — while parking, not destroying, the broad-stack monolith and its campaign. It wins because every step is cheap, every step is decision-changing, no step launders replay R into a live claim (the divergence matrix blocks that by construction), and the expensive machinery it refuses to build — monolith memory work, re-seals, attribution firehoses — demonstrably does not sit between today and a payout.

## RISKS
**1. The survivor book's costs and its liveness are anti-correlated — this is the deepest risk.** Measured commission spares exactly the sleeves that have never been seen to fire live (metals/index core: zero placements from any train-validated sleeve in 38 days, B99b) and kills the sleeves that demonstrably generate (JPY/FX). If G4 returns "naturally quiet", the cost-true MC must be rebuilt on *measured* generation frequency, and a book that rarely trades reaches payout targets slowly or never; if G4 returns "generation broken", the validated book has never run live at all and the premise inverts into a repair project. Either branch can stretch this architecture's calendar far past what "payout-first" implies.

**2. The re-cost band may not settle.** If the F39 ambiguity (spread-only vs commission-inclusive class map) straddles zero for the load-bearing sleeves, the "cheap" ledger re-cost decides nothing and the decision falls through to the slower S3 replay measurement — the architecture's speed premise partially fails.

**3. Ledger provenance.** The re-cost binds to `D4_COMBINED_TRADE_LEDGER.jsonl` and per-sleeve ledgers in the route directory; CYCLE62's accepted numbers live on another branch (`origin/live-handoff-2026-06-15` @ `98da29d2a`). If the series CYCLE62 consumed (n=1679 core8) does not reconcile row-for-row against the in-tree ledgers, the restatement inherits a provenance dispute and must first re-derive the exact consumed series.

**4. One regime, two governors.** The forward-shadow window is June-July 2026 only, and Session F's strongest divergence row (42 % of live signals fired on one account only) plus the governor asymmetry (B67) mean "the survivor book" is really two per-account books. The dossier carries per-account MC, but a canary sized off a 5-week single-regime shadow can still be wrong in ways no gate here catches — only small size and tripwires bound it.

**5. Payout-first defers the charter's compounding asset.** The feature/label stores, trained models, and daily learning loop wait behind the canary. If the survivor book proves marginal, this architecture will have spent its calendar on a thin edge while the learning moat idled. Mitigation is partial: the shadow lane and capture-format fixes are shared infrastructure either way.

**6. The whole path hangs on OD-3, which asks the owner to reverse a decision he made two days ago.** The honest ground is that the dissolution evidence (B63/B99b: the fortnight measured neither stack; the loss was 76.4 % candidate-book) postdates OD-1 — but if the owner keeps the broad candidate, this architecture demotes to challenger-baseline work (B98's framing) and its payout claim lapses. The architecture is explicit about this rather than assuming consent.

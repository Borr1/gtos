## THESIS
GTOS today spends its compute proving things about the one policy family with uniformly negative economics (broad V4: four negative January cells [VERIFIED SECOND_AUDIT.md:118-128], −0.25 R/fill native live, KIAP 1W/20L) through a 96,047-line monolith [MEASURED] whose CPU is 60.6% proof machinery, while the only family with positive long-horizon validation (the W7 book, CYCLE62 +0.0815 R/trade — contaminated by a missing commission term, F38, and a wrong-sign erosion credit, F39 [VERIFIED GATE_G1B_RECEIPT.md:736-737]) is the declared live surface and now has a validated replay decision port with zero disagreements over 617 unit-bearing live cycles [VERIFIED IMPLEMENTATION_STATE.md B92]. This architecture optimises for the shortest honest distance to a defensible activation decision by deleting everything that measures the wrong family: retire broad V4 as a trading policy (bank its January factorial as three design claims), close the sealed campaign, delete the monolith and the 219,469 unreachable lines [VERIFIED OVERENGINEERING_AND_DELETION_MAP.md:168], and rebuild replay as a ~3-5k-line bar-level kernel around the book that delegates to live code for both generation and decision — so fidelity is structural, arms cost minutes and megabytes instead of 16.5 hours and 867 MB, and every remaining engineering hour lands on the four questions that actually gate payout: does generation work (G4), is the book positive at measured costs (F38/F39 repair + revalidation), does replay match live forward (digital twin), and is the canary package safe. It wins because deletion is the only move that simultaneously fixes the two-stacks fork, the memory wall, the proof-CPU inversion, and the H1/H4/H5 hazard family — all of which are properties of the apparatus, not of the mission.

## RISKS
Stated against myself:

1. **This architecture asks the owner to reverse OD-1, two days after he made it.** He went "full in" on the broad system on 2026-07-25 (SECOND_AUDIT.md:150-153). My evidence case (four negative cells, −0.25 R/fill, KIAP 1W/20L, versus a W7 whose damning fortnight Session E dissolved) is strong but the call is his; if he declines, this architecture degrades to "kernel beside campaign" and its central economy — deleting the monolith — is gone, leaving ~16.5 h/window economics and the ~2-day April/May/March spend intact.
2. **Generation parity is the unproven half, and I have made it the keystone.** B92 validated decisions closed-form and bar-free; generation drags in bar_provider semantics, warm-up state, the conviction store (78.1% of sized cycles), nine day keys, and 16-char comment truncation (F34). If the G4 run shows large mismatch, the "days" estimates become weeks, and the delegation pattern's clean story acquires an asterisk.
3. **The cost table generalises from one 38-day window in one regime.** Commission is contractual and stable; spread and slippage are not (FN XAUUSD p95 $1.31 vs FTMO $0.49; GBPJPY 0.183 R with no floor). A sleeve that survives or dies by <0.05 R in K2 is inside cost-model noise — the sensitivity bands mitigate but cannot adjudicate razor-thin compositions, and I may be forced to say "unknown" exactly where the owner wants an answer.
4. **K2 revalidates on the same 2014-2026 bars the sleeves were mined from.** Fixing F38/F39 removes cost optimism, not selection bias. The banked factorial itself warns that quality selection measured inconclusive. K3's forward weeks are the real test, and they are the one cost I cannot compress — a positive K2 may still die forward.
5. **Deletion of the campaign apparatus is practically irreversible.** Re-execution (as opposed to re-reading) of sealed January arms is gone with the code + H4 path bindings. If a future dispute requires demonstrating the arms run, not just their receipts, that capability was sold for repo hygiene.
6. **Bar-level replay cannot see intraday execution.** I judged tick fidelity second-order from measured clean stops and small slippage — measured on the SAME window as the cost table. If the book's risk profile changes (larger sizes at canary, thinner sessions), gap-through and slippage behavior may not transfer; the shadow reducer's F31 finding (zero gap-through granted, −8.095 R effect) shows exactly how expensive that assumption class can be.

I deliberately did not rest the case on memory: the monolith measures a policy family with negative evidence everywhere — that, not RSS, is the reason it goes. But if OD-R1 reverses broad's status, this risk inverts entirely.
8. **The learning stack is retained on faith.** 6,600 dormant lines kept because the charter names the destination; under my own deletion logic ("which decision does it change?") it changes none today. I keep it because rebuilding it later costs more than its carry; that is a judgment, not a measurement.

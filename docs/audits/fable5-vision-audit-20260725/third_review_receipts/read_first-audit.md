**Amends (five material deltas from the first audit's shape):**
1. **Delegation, not extraction, for the live-book policy.** `SleeveBookPolicy` "re-implements no arithmetic" — it converts the data contract and calls `bridge.evaluate_vnext_ultimate_book_admission`, the exact function `book_engine.py:741-746` calls in production (B91). This is a different construction than `gtos.core`'s "decision logic moves into the pure core" — for code that is already live, wrapping it beats porting it (the live code moved twice during the session; a copy drifts silently).
2. **Size unit is risk-percent, not lots** (`core.py:29-34`): lots are broker/profile-specific and "replay never computes a lot quantity at all" (E1d) — the first audit's `SizedOrder` is amended to `size_risk_pct`; a lots-returning policy "would be claiming a fidelity it does not have."
3. **Candidate generation is deliberately outside the Policy contract** (`core.py:236-241`): the two families generate incomparably; OD-1's addendum fixes the merge at the portfolio level via the trade-intent contract `(symbol, side, entry, stop, size, provenance)` (`FULL_VISION_PLAN.md:63-76`). The first audit's `generate_candidates` stage is per-policy, not core.
4. **Unit-level risk structure is first-class** (`PolicyUnit`, `core.py:36-42,152-168`): flattening a correlated cluster-day unit to per-trade sizes "would discard the correlation structure the governor's gross cap actually binds on" — a concept absent from the first audit's pipeline.
5. **The ports triple is still not realised.** No Clock/Broker/Sink adapter modules exist; `AccountDayState.cycle` (an opaque per-cycle mapping of broker-derived facts, `core.py:130-146`) substitutes for a Broker port at the decision level; the 15 `PLACEMENT_GATES` (`sleeve_book.py:116-132`) are explicitly excluded (need a broker tick + durable ledger). Diagnostics ride in the decision rather than through a Sink.

**Not yet validated:** `BroadV4Policy` does not exist ("the sibling lane… is not running", B90); nothing has been attached at the v4:90497 seam; **gate G2 is not met**. Session H validated its policy against *live packets*, not by driving the replay loop — a stronger fidelity claim about live, but not yet the Stage-2 falsification experiment for the V4 decomposition. Also material for the review: **B98** — OD-1 reversed the brief's premise (W7 is the deactivated challenger, not the activation candidate), and the module's rationale improves (only replay representation of the declared-live surface; walk-forward baseline; the instrument Session E needs); and **B99b** — over 38 live days the book's 145 placements included **zero** from the five train-validated core sleeves and 43.4 % from the two registry-falsified sleeves, so the OD-1 drawdown "is not evidence about the W7 core book's edge."

**Net verdict for the review author:** wave-2 evidence **validates the seam-and-port concept and the policy-plural amendment, and amends the construction**: policy = delegation wrapper where live code exists; risk-pct units; generation excluded; unit structure preserved; Clock/Broker/Sink deferred.

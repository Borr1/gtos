## 1. What the implemented permutation rule actually does

`perm_null.block_permutation_test` partitions the series into contiguous blocks **anchored at index 0**
— blocks {0–2}, {3–5}, …, {27–29}, {30} (ten 3-day blocks plus a 1-day remainder pinned to the series
end) — and multiplies each block by an independent ±1, 10,000 times, with `stat="mean"`. One common
grid phase (0) is shared by all blocks and all permutations; no rotation is ever drawn. The null
statistic is

  m(s) = (1/n) Σ_b s_b B_b(φ),  s_b ∈ {±1},  B_b(φ) = the φ-anchored block sums.

## 2. Rotation invariance — NO, and it decides the verdict

**Algebra.** The observed statistic (the mean) is rotation-invariant; the null is not. With
μ = x̄ = 7.5193, L_b the block lengths and C_b(φ) = B_b(φ) − L_b·μ the centred block sums:

  Σ_b B_b(φ)² = Σ_b C_b(φ)² + 4μ·(μ − x_last(φ)) + 91·μ²   (verified to 0.00e+00 residual)

The null variance of m is Σ B_b(φ)²/n². Both terms that carry φ — Σ C_b(φ)² (which within-block
covariance the grid happens to capture) and x_last(φ) (which day lands in the 1-day remainder block) —
change under rotation, so the null law depends on the arbitrary anchor. The rule *implicitly* appeals to
stationarity (its own bootstrap arm wraps the same series circularly), yet it samples exactly one member
of the 31-member rotation orbit. Note also that the orbit cannot be repaired by enlarging the group:
the group generated by rotations plus fixed-phase block flips is the **full** per-day sign-flip group
(the 31 cyclic length-3 indicator vectors span F₂³¹), whose null destroys the autocorrelation the block
construction exists to preserve. Fixed-phase block flips are a valid group; the *choice of phase* is the
unexamined degree of freedom.

**Exact measurement** (the 2¹¹ = 2048-atom support is fully enumerable — no Monte Carlo needed):

- p_exact by rotation r = 0…30: **min 1/2048 = 0.000488, median 4/2048, max 10/2048 = 0.004883** (10×).
- At the BH rank-1 bar p ≤ α/m = 0.0016949: **14 of 31 rotations ADMIT, 17 REJECT.** The implemented
  anchor (phase 0) gives 4/2048 = 0.001953 → q = 0.1152 → REJECT.
- The razor edge: at phase 0 the qualifying flip-subsets are exactly {}, {B4}, {B8}, {B4,B8}, because
  only two block sums are negative — B8 = −3.875 and **B4 = −0.0692, one percent of the day-level sd**
  (Apr 22–24: −1.111 + 0.757 + 0.285). Had that near-zero block sum been +0.069, the count is 3 →
  p = 3/2048 → q = 0.0864 → **ADMIT**. The published REJECT hinged on where an arbitrary grid dropped a
  block boundary across three April days.

**Verdict: the common-fixed-phase rule is NOT rotation-invariant; it under-samples the null orbit
(1 phase of 31, 2048 atoms of ~63k), and on this data the sampling choice is verdict-determining.**

## 3. The support cap and the seed lottery

- Support: **2048 distinct assignments** (2048 distinct atom values — no ties). 10,000 MC draws resample
  them; the gate's own `p_floor` telemetry (0.000588) already said so. Deeper consequence: near the bar,
  q is quantised in steps of 59/2048 = 0.0288 — the only achievable q's around 0.10 are 0.0864 and
  0.1152. **No q near 0.10 is even expressible under this rule.**
- MC seed sweep at the gate's n_perm = 10⁴ (block 3): p = 0.0026 (seed 20260729, published), 0.0023
  (seed 1), 0.0018 (seed 2), **0.0016 (seed 3 → q = 0.094 → ADMIT)**. The published verdict flips
  across the seed at the gate's own permutation count. At 10⁶ draws the MC converges to 0.00204; the
  exact value is 4/2048 = 0.001953 (REJECT, q = 0.115).

## 4. Why the implemented null is wrong twice, in opposite directions

1. **Signal contamination (conservative under H1).** The rule flips *uncentred* blocks, so
   E[B_b] = L_b·μ and the 91μ² term above contributes **64.8 % of the null variance**
   (5145.1 of 7524.4; null sd 2.798 vs 1.659 centred). The candidate's own edge widens the null it is
   judged against. This is why the gate's permutation arm (0.0026) sat 26× above its own bootstrap arm.
2. **Dependence leak (anti-conservative under H0).** The spec's `block_length_auto` decorrelation rule
   wanted **L = 7** at ρ̂ = 0.489 (`ceil(ln 0.01/ln 0.489)`); the `min_blocks = 8` cap — which exists
   only because the sign-flip family needs 2^B ≥ 2⁸ support, i.e. the chosen null family's discreteness
   — forced **L = 3**. Measured under a mean-zero Gaussian AR(1) with the series' own lag-1 (200,000
   sims; independently re-verified with a fresh implementation at 50,000): at the decision bar
   α = 0.0016949 the original rule's true size is **0.0064 (3.8×)**; a naively centred fixed-phase
   variant is **0.0489 (29×)**; the CBB at L=3 is 6.3× at the 1 % level. Every resampling arm the gate
   composes is anti-conservative at this block length under its own assumed dependence class.

Net on this data the two defects resolve conservative (exact 0.00195 vs calibrated 0.0001–0.0013 below)
— but by luck of regime, not by construction.

## 5. The corrected null

**Why not just fix phase + centring?** Because the fix collides with a hard reachability bound:
Σ|x_i − x̄| = 189.92 < n·x̄ = 233.10, so **no sign assignment of centred blocks — at any block length,
any phase — can reach the observed statistic**. The centred sign-flip's exact p is a hard 0 with its
most extreme atom at 72.8 % of the observed value (63,488-atom full enumeration at L=3; MC 3×10⁶: zero
exceedances). The face-value resampling numbers that replace it are exact but incredible:
CBB(L=3) tail = **9.0e-12** (rigorous FFT bracket [8.99, 9.00]e-12), CBB(L=7) = **0 of 28,629,151**
exact combinations. A 31-point dependent series cannot certify 1e-12; §4's size measurements say
precisely that these tails are inflated by orders of magnitude. Any of them would "ADMIT" — dishonestly.

**Construction actually used — direct finite-sample calibration.** Fit the dependence the data show,
impose H0 exactly, and compute the tail of the *studentised* weighted mean by simulation:

- three **independent fold segments** of 11/11/9 days (the concatenation seams are physically real —
  April day 1 does not follow January day 30; circular wrapping was itself a mild model violation);
- **within-fold AR(1)** persistence — the measured within-fold ACF is 0.412, **0.015**, 0.141, … (dead
  beyond lag 1; pooled within-fold ρ̂ = 0.372, SE 0.175);
- **per-fold scale** (sd 4.57 / 7.31 / 6.93) — the weighting is inherited through the fold scales;
- innovations **Gaussian or resampled standardized AR residuals** (skew −0.15 kept);
- fold-level regime excess: **measured absent** — between-fold variance of means 6.35 vs ≈ 7 predicted
  from within-fold noise alone, so no month-effect term is added (month-regime risk is charged by the
  spec's stability/robustness gates, which this candidate passes 3/3);
- statistic: T = x̄/(s/√31) = 6.0405; p = P_H0(T ≥ T_obs). No block, no phase, no support cap —
  rotation-invariance is moot because nothing is anchored; validity is exact *given the model*, so all
  model risk is made explicit as a ρ grid and the **primary p is the max across variants** (the spec's
  own `both_conservative` philosophy applied where the risk actually lives).

**Results** (2.1M sims per variant = 3 seeds × 700k; per-seed spreads shown in the JSON):

| variant (ρ, innovations) | p (pooled) | per-seed spread | q = 59p | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.197 (within −1 SE), gauss / resid | 1.0e-5 / 1.8e-5 | ±2e-6 | 0.0006 / 0.0011 | ADMIT |
| **0.372 (measured within)**, gauss / resid | 9.8e-5 / **1.57e-4** | ±1e-5 | 0.0058 / **0.0092** | ADMIT |
| 0.489 (full-series lag1, conservative), gauss / resid | 4.9e-4 / 6.6e-4 | ±3e-5 | 0.029 / 0.039 | ADMIT |
| **0.548 (within +1 SE), resid — the primary (max)** | **1.303e-3** | 1.27/1.34/1.30e-3 | **0.0769** | **ADMIT** |

**Corrected p = 0.00130** (worst defensible fitted variant; at the measured dependence it is 1.6e-4).

## 7. Sensitivity and fragility

**Original rule** (exact, no MC noise):

| block (spec 3, ±50 % ⇒ 2 and 4) | p at phase 0 | p across 31 rotations | verdict at phase 0 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | 0.000275 | 0.000107 – 0.000748 | **ADMIT** (q = 0.016) |
| 3 | 0.001953 | 0.000488 – 0.004883 | REJECT (q = 0.115) — 14/31 rotations ADMIT |
| 4 | 0.007813 | 0.003906 – 0.015625 | REJECT |
| 5 / 6 / 7 | 0.0078 / 0.0156 / 0.0313 | rising; support 2⁷/2⁶/2⁵ collapsing | REJECT |

Plus the seed lottery (§3: 1 of 4 seeds ADMITs at the gate's own n_perm). The implemented rule's verdict
is **seed-, phase- and block-dependent** in the immediate neighbourhood of its published configuration.

**Corrected null:** no block/phase/seed sensitivity by construction (per-seed spread ≤ ±3 % of p at the
argmax variant); the binding knob is the dependence model. Flip scan (worst innovation law, 1.2M sims
per point): ρ = 0.548 → q 0.077 ADMIT; **ρ = 0.60 → q 0.134 REJECT**; 0.65 → 0.234; 0.70 → 0.395. The
ADMIT therefore holds until within-fold lag-1 persistence ≈ **0.56–0.60**, i.e. ≥ ~1.2 SE above the
measured 0.372 and ≈ 0.1 above even the fold-shift-conflating full-series 0.489.

**Flags** (also in the JSON):

1. The published REJECT is one draw from a rule whose verdict flips across seed, rotation phase, and
   block length — it should not be read as a stable property of the evidence.
2. The corrected ADMIT carries a 23 % q-margin at the worst fitted variant and flips at ρ ≳ 0.6;
   an ADMIT this dependent on the dependence model warrants conservative sizing and a pre-registered
   re-read on the next unread window.
3. Low-df constructions REJECT (batch-means t B=4: p 0.042; B=5: 0.008; fold-mean t, n=3: 0.027) —
   at df 3–4 no candidate can certify p ≤ 0.0017, so these are resolution limits, not counter-evidence;
   the Kish-t at ρ = 0.489 (p 0.0028, REJECT) extrapolates AR(1) persistence to infinite lags, which the
   measured within-fold lag-2 ACF (0.015 vs 0.239 implied) does not support, and its finite-sample,
   seam-respecting replacement is the calibrated ρ = 0.489 variant (p 0.00066, ADMIT).
4. Face-value resampling tails from this 31-point series (9e-12 … 3.5e-8) overstate the evidence by
   4–9 orders of magnitude; treat any future gate p ≲ 1e-4 from a series this short as a floor artifact.

## 8. Answers to the question, compressed

- **Rotation-invariant?** **No.** The common fixed circular phase makes the null law a function of an
  arbitrary anchor (through B_b(φ)); exactly enumerated, p spans 10× across the 31 rotations and the
  verdict flips at 14 of them. The null space is under-sampled (1 of 31 phases; 2048 of ~63k atoms).
- **Original p:** published MC 0.0025997 (n_perm 10,000, seed 20260729); seeds {20260729,1,2,3} →
  {0.0026, 0.0023, 0.0018, 0.0016} — the spread crosses the bar; exact enumeration **4/2048 = 0.0019531**
  (support intrinsically capped at 2¹¹ = 2048; MC floor 0.000588 as the receipt itself reported).
- **Corrected p:** **0.00130** — max over fitted-dependence variants of the exactly-calibrated
  studentised-mean tail (argmax ρ = 0.548, residual innovations; 3 seeds × 700k sims each, spread
  1.27–1.34e-3; at the measured dependence 1.57e-4). Corrected resampling arms for the record:
  CBB L=7 exact 0/28,629,151, MC 3×10⁶ zero exceedances; centred sign-flip exact 0 (unreachable support).
- **q at 59:** original exact 0.1152 (REJECT); **corrected 0.0769 → ADMIT at α = 0.10** with every other
  receipt predicate passing.
- **Fragility:** original verdict unstable in seed/phase/block; corrected ADMIT stable in those but
  flips at within-fold ρ ≳ 0.6 (measured 0.372 ± 0.175) and is invisible to df ≤ 4 constructions.

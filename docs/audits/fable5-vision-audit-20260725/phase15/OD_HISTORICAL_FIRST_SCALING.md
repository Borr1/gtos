# Owner directive — HISTORICAL-FIRST SCALING (2026-07-31)

**Borhen, 2026-07-31, verbatim intent:** "data must never be a reason of not doing anything,
we have all the data we need and if dont have it we can simply fetch it … waiting for months
and days in live is just stupid so the scaling and going all the way must be on the
historical data and whatever we confirm in historical data we push live with no fear, im
tired of this conservative passivness."

This is a standing directive with the same authority as the training-lane ratification. It
changes five things and deliberately leaves four untouched.

## What changes

1. **The live stream is a VETO, never the clock.** Promotion, scaling and arming decisions
   rest on sealed-gate historical evidence. The live record can *block* (a stop rule firing,
   a contradiction between live behaviour and the historical contract) — it is never the
   thing we wait on to accumulate significance. Any rule whose binding clock is a live fill
   count measured in months is **mis-derived under this directive** and gets restated.
   First instance: `mx_btcusd`'s P1 promotion ceiling (+34 R over 60 fills ≈ 51 calendar
   months) — Session CE restates it historical-primary with a live veto.
2. **"Missing data" is an action item, never a verdict.** A gate that returns
   NOT_EVALUABLE for sample reasons obliges the orchestrator to price and execute the fetch
   (the vp_euidx_pocgrav M1 backfill is being executed the same hour as this record). A
   sleeve may only be called data-blocked in a result doc if the fetch was priced and is
   impossible.
3. **A measured, controls-clean improvement to an armed sleeve must not sleep behind a
   default-off flag.** Built-but-unarmed is a DEFECT state with an owner-visible clock, not
   a resting state. Current inventory (all with clean controls, all unarmed): AY's
   spread-geometry floor (REPAIR on both `sub_mid_dn_revert` +0.426 R/day and
   `sub_xvol_pullback` +0.264 R/day), the ratified entry-hour lever (+0.063…+0.141 R/trade
   net, 38/42 members), BA's weekend policy. Session CE builds all three to the ceremony
   step; the orchestrator executes.
4. **Historical confirmation → live activation without a live probation clock.** What the
   sealed gate admits and the owner arms goes live at its incubation weight immediately;
   weight steps up on *historical-milestone* evidence (new folds, band robustness, regime
   coverage) plus the absence of a live veto — not on live sample size.
5. **The robustness gate gets a research challenge, not blind deference.** Kill-on-fold-
   concentration reads regime-dependence as fragility. The training lane owes an answer to
   "condition on the regime instead of killing the sleeve" (filed to the repair queue as a
   trainer-session item; `metals_softband` is the test case).

## What does NOT change, and why — this is the part that makes "no fear" rational

1. **The sealed admission rule** (`CANDIDATE_BOOK_V1`, all-declared, sealed `B_balanced`
   α = 0.10, RECORDED with AN's conditions) stays frozen. It is not conservatism; it is
   what makes "confirmed on historical data" a sentence with content. Loosen it and
   "confirmed" means "fit to noise" — and then live becomes the discovery instrument again,
   which is the slow path this directive exists to kill.
2. **TEST inviolability** (March 2026, the blackouts, the live forward stream as virgin
   test). Same reason: the no-fear push needs something it never touched to be honest.
3. **Token, seal and ceremony discipline.** Fearless about edges, never careless about the
   broker path.
4. **The risk dial, allocation profile, and every arming remain Borhen's ceremony.**

## The clarification that dissolves half the worry

**The admission gate does not decide what trades — Borhen does.** Four of the five sleeves
armed on FTMO today never passed the sealed gate; they are `OWNER_RISK_ACCEPTED`, armed on
his explicit instruction, and that is a legitimate basis the incubation registry records in
its own vocabulary. The gate prices the *label* ("this is a statistically defensible edge"),
which is what protects the estate from believing its own noise. It has never once been the
thing standing between a sleeve and the live book.
